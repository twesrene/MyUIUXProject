Hello this is an app that i make in year 2025-2026 during high school 
It is a Pinterest-inspired app to find & save perfect gifts.

Demo Video
(https://youtu.be/d5_LVYkVuSc)

User Flow
- Login
- Home Feed 
- Search Gift
- Save to Board
- Gift Detail
- Calender (to remind you about your friend's birthday)

Designed in Adobe XD

Apps Used : Adobe XD , Ibis Paint X , Pinterest
